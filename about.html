About Yael

I write letters from a small farm on a long hill — about faith, fences, kitchen tables, animals with opinions, and the slow work of becoming a person. The letters began on borrowed ground, posted into a feed that scrolled them away by morning. This is their home now: one quiet place, owned outright, where nothing scrolls away.

If you’re new: start with any letter at all. They were written one at a time and they’re happiest read that way. If you’ve been reading all along — you know who you are, and these pages were built for you.

— Yael
