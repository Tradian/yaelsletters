A short introduction

About Yael

Turning believers back to a whole-Bible perspective — to find the true and deep understanding of the amazing, beautiful Hebrew love story again.

First, the question everyone asks: the goat. Yael means “goat” in Hebrew — that’s it. No hidden symbol, no secret message. Just a name, and a fitting one for someone who was led out to the wilderness.

Yael is a pen name. Not for mystery’s sake, but because this is not my project — it’s His — and I want all the glory to go to YHWH alone. Who am I? No one important; no one you would ever have heard of. Just someone who loves Jesus with all her heart and wants to share Him with others.

I came out of mainstream church years ago with a broken heart. YHWH called me out to the wilderness to remove everything between Him and me, and to heal me — and He is so faithful: He met me right where, when, and how I needed Him. For five years, most of what I did was study His Word, and I fell more in love with it every day. Last summer He put it on my heart to start writing. So here I am.

I am a whole-Bible believer. I believe it is one continuous, beautiful, amazing story from Genesis to Revelation — a love story, a war manual, and covenant documents all wrapped into one. It is the story of His people: the Commonwealth of Israel, the native-born and the grafted-in. We are One Family, One People, One Bride, One Body in Him. If I had to choose a denomination, I wouldn’t. I am a follower of The Way, the Ancient Path — a Natsarim.

I will be the first to tell you I do not have all the answers. I am on the same learning journey as you are in this life. Thank you for being here — I appreciate you immensely.

— Yael
