Before the Rooster

by Yael

Forty short readings for the hour when the kettle is on and the day hasn’t made its demands yet. Small enough to keep by the stove; deep enough to carry till evening.

Get the ebook Or the paper copy

A few pages, for a taste

From the opening

Before the rooster — before the phone, before the first should of the day lands on your chest — there is a small, unclaimed quiet. This book is for that.

Forty mornings. Keep it by the kettle.

Reading I

From the middle

Denial does not only happen at midnight. Peter’s failing ran right into the morning; the rooster crows into the daylight, too. Mercifully, grace keeps the same hours — it was a breakfast on the shore that put him back together.

Reading XIX

The last page

You will not always rise before the rooster. Some mornings the day begins without asking your permission. On those, this is the whole reading: He is not waiting to be impressed. He is glad you came at all.

Yael
