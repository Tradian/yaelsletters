About this book

The Counting of the Omer is the forty-nine-day count Scripture marks between Firstfruits and Shavuot (Leviticus 23). This devotional walks those days one at a time — a short, unhurried reading for each, in the same whole-Bible spirit as the letters.

The full-colour PDF is free to download and keep, images and all. The print edition is on Amazon — its images are black-and-white to keep printing affordable, and colour versions of any image are available on request.

Read it free, or hold it in your hands — whichever blesses you most.
