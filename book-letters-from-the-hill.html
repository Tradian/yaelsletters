Letters from the Hill

by Yael

The first two years of letters, in the order the seasons wrote them — with a few pages that never went out at all. Long on purpose, backed by hours in the Word.

Get the ebook Or the paper copy

A few pages, for a taste

From the opening

I almost didn’t write this one. The kettle was on, the house still dark, and every reason to stay quiet sat down at the table with me. But the Word doesn’t keep banker’s hours — and neither, it turns out, does grace.

So here is the first letter. Longer than it needs to be, the way the good ones always are.

1

From the middle

We have made patience a small word — a thing for waiting rooms and slow checkout lines. Scripture means something heavier by it: the strength to stay when leaving would be easier, to keep the lamp trimmed through a long night you did not choose.

Read this one slowly. I had to.

138

The last page

If you carry one thing out of two years of letters, carry this: you are not too late, and you are not too far. The hill is still there. The light still climbs it every morning, whether or not anyone is watching.

Come up when you can.

Yael
