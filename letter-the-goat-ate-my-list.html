A letter · March 2026

The Goat Ate My List

Dear friend — I had the whole spring planned on one sheet of paper. Seed orders down the left margin, fence repairs down the right, and across the top, underlined twice, the words this year, on time. I set it on the porch rail while I carried feed, and you already know where this is going.

She didn’t even hurry. That’s the part I keep returning to. She walked over as if invited, took the corner of April in her mouth, and looked straight at me while the whole season disappeared like a communion wafer. I stood there holding a feed bucket, watching my plans become, very literally, her breakfast.

I was angry for about a minute. Then I sat down on the step and laughed until the dog came to check on me. Because here is the truth I would not have written to you otherwise: the list was a fence I had built around the spring, and I was already living pressed against the inside of it, in February, out of breath.

The seeds went in late. The fence got mended when the ground was ready and not before. And the spring came anyway — it always comes anyway — not because I scheduled it but because Someone keeps a calendar far kinder than mine.

So this month I am practicing planning in pencil. Light lines. Wide margins. Room in the corner of every page for a goat.

— Yael
