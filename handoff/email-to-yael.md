## ○ What I need from you (about 20 minutes)

1. Create a free account at **payhip.com** (use your email).
2. Connect **Stripe** under Settings → Payments — that's where your money lands.
3. Add Product → Digital → upload the ebook file, add the title, a short blurb, and the cover.
4. Set the price (e.g. $4.99). *Optional:* turn on PDF stamping so each copy carries the buyer's email — gentle anti-piracy.
5. Publish, then copy the product link.
6. Do the same for the second book, and **send me both links**. I'll paste them in and your buttons go live.
