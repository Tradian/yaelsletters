The fine print, kept honest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Draft for review. This policy is written to match how the site actually handles data, but it is a template, not legal advice. Please have it reviewed by a qualified professional, and confirm the bracketed details, before the site collects information from the public.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Yael’s Letters (“we,” “us”) collects, why, how it is handled, and the choices you have. We collect as little as possible and never sell your information.

Who is responsible

Yael’s Letters is operated by [Our Fellow Brands / legal entity] (the “data controller”). For any privacy question or request, contact us at ian@ourfellowbrands.com. Postal mail: [mailing address].

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you subscribe to the letters.
  • Comments you choose to post (your name as given, and the comment text), if and when commenting is enabled.
  • Order and donation details, if you buy a book or give support. Payments are handled entirely by our payment providers — we never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) that our hosting and service providers log automatically to deliver and secure the site.

How we use it

  • To send you the letters you asked for, and to manage that subscription.
  • To display and moderate comments, where enabled.
  • To fulfil book orders and process donations through our providers.
  • To keep the site running, secure, and free of abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Legal bases (for visitors in the UK/EU)

  • Consent — for the newsletter. You can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Contract — to provide a book or product you purchase.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve the site, and to moderate the community.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers (“processors”) to run the site. Each handles only what it needs to, under its own privacy terms:

  • Resend — sends the letters and stores the subscriber list.
  • Cloudflare — receives the sign-up form submission and passes it securely to Resend.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts and serves the website.
  • Stripe and/or Buy Me a Coffee — process payments and donations.
  • [Comments provider] — stores and displays comments, if enabled.
  • Google Fonts — serves the page typefaces; this involves a request to Google, which may receive your IP address.

Some of these providers are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there under appropriate safeguards.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not follow you around the web. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

How long we keep it

We keep your email for as long as you are subscribed; when you unsubscribe, you are removed from the active list. Order and donation records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations. Comments remain until you or we remove them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or to stop using it, at any time. You can unsubscribe from any email using the link in it. To make a request, email ian@ourfellowbrands.com. If you are in the UK/EU you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use reputable providers and sensible safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take care to limit what we collect and who can access it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the current version.

Contact

Questions? Write to ian@ourfellowbrands.com.
