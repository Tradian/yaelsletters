Support the Letters

The letters are free, and they will stay free. And it’s just little ol’ me here — one woman with a day job, a family, and other commitments I love — writing from my own “spare” time and resources.

Every letter is backed by hours of research and study, written in the quietest hours of the day, in the hope that it enriches your relationship with our Heavenly Father. If they have, and you find yourself wanting to help carry them, you can. It makes a real difference, and I notice every single one.

But hear me on this: this is not my project — it’s His. All the glory goes to YHWH alone.

— Yael
